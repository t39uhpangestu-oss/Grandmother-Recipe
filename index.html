Grandmother's Recipe

Short description: A family recipe card was written by Great-Grandma Sri, and it has been passed down ever since. Catch the ingredients in the bowl. Each finished dish opens one of her handwritten memories.

How to play

  • The recipe card at the top lists the ingredients you need. Ingredients fall from above.
  • Move the bowl under the ingredients you need. Each one you catch is crossed off the card, and a memory appears.
  • Catch every ingredient on the card to finish the dish. It is served with love, and a memory card from Great-Grandma Sri opens. Tap KEEP COOKING to continue.
  • Memory cards start once you reach 100 points. There are 50 cards, and they appear in random order.
  • Catching the wrong ingredient costs one heart. So does letting a needed ingredient fall past the bowl.
  • You have 3 hearts. When they run out, the family dinner is over. Every 100 points, the recipes grow longer and the ingredients fall faster.
  • The house changes to another room at random every 100 points.

Controls

  • Touch: drag left or right to move the bowl.
  • Mouse: drag left or right with the button held down.
  • Keyboard: Left and Right arrows. Space to start.
